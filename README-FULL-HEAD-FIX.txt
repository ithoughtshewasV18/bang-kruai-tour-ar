WAT CHALO FULL-HEAD PHONE FIX

The portrait phone camera was still being cropped to fill the tall screen.
That removed part of one side of the head before the AI mask was applied.

This version:
- shows the complete selfie camera frame;
- centers the person;
- scales the phone selfie layer to 94% so both sides of the head remain visible;
- keeps Wat Chalo filling the whole background;
- removes the extra blur on the face and hair;
- retains more edge pixels around hair and ears.
