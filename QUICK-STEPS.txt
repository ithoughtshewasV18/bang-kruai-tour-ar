QUICK CHECKLIST

1. Upload this folder to GitHub.
2. Enable GitHub Pages: main / root.
3. Open the HTTPS page on your phone.
4. Tap Start AR Camera.
5. Show assets/demo-marker.png on another screen.
6. Point the phone at it.
7. Tap the white shutter button.
8. Save the AR photo.

CUSTOMIZE:
9. Compile the full Wat Chalo postcard with the official MindAR compiler.
10. Save the result as targets/wat-chalo.mind.
11. Replace assets/ar-overlay.png with your transparent Wat Chalo PNG.
12. Change imageTargetSrc in index.html to ./targets/wat-chalo.mind.
13. Link the GitHub Pages URL to your Google Sites camera icon.
